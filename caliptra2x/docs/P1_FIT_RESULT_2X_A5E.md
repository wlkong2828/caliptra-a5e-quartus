# P1 fit gate: Caliptra 2.x core-only passive top on Agilex 5E A5EC065BB32AE6S

**Result: synthesis PASSED. The design does NOT fit on logic. The ALM estimate is 126 % of the device.**
Memory, DSP and register counts all fit.

## Setup (`quartus_a5e_2x/`)

- **Tools and part:** Quartus Prime Pro 25.3.0 b109 (Docker `alterafpga/quartuspro-v25.3:agilex5`, SW-AGILEX-5E fixed license). Analysis & Synthesis only.
- **Top:** `rtl_2x/caliptra_passive_top.sv`, the 2.x core-only wrapper. It wraps caliptra-rtl v2.1.2 `49370266`, with `CALIPTRA_MODE_SUBSYSTEM` undefined, no internal TRNG, and `MLDSA_MASKING` on (upstream default).
- **Tie-offs:** the AXI manager (DMA) side is tied off, as are strap_ss_*, ss_debug_intent, ss_ocp_lock_en, recovery_*, obf_uds_seed and field_entropy.
- **Inputs left live:** obf key, CSR HMAC key and security_state are top ports. They are virtual pins, not constants, so no logic gets optimized away.
- **Pins:** all I/O are VIRTUAL_PIN except clk and jtag_tck.
- **Memories:** all are inferred RAM via `syn_src/` overrides (zero-init caliptra_sram; abr_1r1w*_ram rewritten with unpacked arrays):
  - mailbox 256 KiB (65536x39);
  - ROM 96 KiB (12288x64, with `fw_2x/caliptra-rom.hex`);
  - ICCM and DCCM 256 KiB each (4 banks of 16384x39);
  - the 10 Adams-Bridge memories.
- **QSF:** generated by `gen_qsf.py` from `ref/agilex7-2x-phaseA/caliptra_passive_top.f`. Settings: SV-2005 parser, MFCU, `SYNTHESIS=1`, `FPGA_SYN=1`, NUM_PARALLEL_PROCESSORS 2.
- **Run:** `QPAR=2 nohup setsid ./run_syn_docker.sh > run.out 2>&1 &`

So this column is **not** the same top as the 2.x column, which is the core-only `caliptra_passive_top`. For a like-for-like comparison, the 1.x core-only wrapper (`quartus_a5e/`, `caliptra_a5e.syn.summary`) estimates 141,256 / 222,400 ALMs (64 %), 142,373 registers and 47 DSP post-merge.

- **Over by:** 57,285 ALMs at 126 %. To fit at a comfortable ~85 % utilization, about 90k ALMs (≈ 32 %) would have to come out.
- **How reliable the estimate is:** "ALMs needed" is a pre-fit estimate. The fitter's ALM packing usually lands within about ±10–15 % of it. Even a 20 % packing gain gives ≈ 224k ALMs, which is still ≥ 100 %. **Verdict: doesn't fit** (not marginal).
- **M20K estimate:** for each RAM, I took the cheapest of the 512x40, 1Kx20 and 2Kx10 modes:
  - mbox 128, ROM 42, ICCM+DCCM 256, ABR 57, ECC 4, SHA3 fifo 2 (MLAB-able).
  - That totals about 484 M20K, roughly 30 % of 1,611.

What drives the overshoot:

- **The ML-DSA masked NTT.** `hybrid_bf2x2` alone holds 218k FFs. Most of these are masking/pipeline delay registers that cannot become M20K/MLAB shift registers. With the 4 FFs per ALM limit they force many ALMs.
- **Growth outside Adams-Bridge.** AES and SHA3 are new. Key vault grew +18k ALUTs (23 slots), VeeR +20k (larger CCMs and 2.x config), and soc_ifc +10k (AXI + DMA).

## Recommendation

1. **Hardware target for full 2.x: Agilex 7** (plan option A).
   - A5E065 is already the largest Agilex 5 E-series part, so there is no bigger Agilex 5 to move to.
   - AGF/AGI 014 (~487k ALMs) would be at ~57 % on this estimate; 022/027 gives more margin.
   - Reuse this flow by changing FAMILY/DEVICE in gen_qsf.py.
2. **If the board must stay A5E065:** keep Caliptra 1.x in hardware, and develop the 2.x glue/firmware in Verilator + sw-emulator (option B).
3. **Optional experiment, only if A5E065 is a hard constraint:** synthesize a reduced variant with `MLDSA_MASKING` undefined and/or `RV_FPGA_OPTIMIZE` defined. This removes SCA masking, which is a security trade-off and not production-representative. It would show whether the masked NTT FFs (~218k) are what pushes the design over.
